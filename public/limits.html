What it proves, and what it doesn't.

A record is evidence about a process. Here is exactly how far that evidence goes.

It can show

  • Checkpoints were made in this order, and none were altered afterwards.
  • How much text was typed, pasted and deleted, and when.
  • That a revealed version matches a checkpoint, with a Merkle proof.

It cannot show

  • That a person did not read AI text and retype it.
  • Who was at the keyboard.
  • Writing done outside the AuthorTrail editor.

Privacy

The server stores hashes and counts only. Each fingerprint is salted with a secret kept in your browser, so nobody can guess your text from it.

Timestamps

Checkpoint times come from the server's clock. For stronger proof, download a document's anchor digest from /api/docs/ID/anchor and stamp it with a public service such as OpenTimestamps.
