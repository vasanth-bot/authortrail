Security and privacy

What AuthorTrail stores, how records are protected, and where the limits are.

What is stored

Stored

  • Document titles and timestamps
  • Salted fingerprints of your work
  • Counts: typed, pasted, deleted, active time
  • Hashes of your owner key and connection code

Never stored

  • Your text or code
  • Keystrokes or screenshots
  • The salt that protects fingerprints (it stays on your device)
  • Your AI coach draft, which is passed on and not saved

How records are protected

  • Tamper evidence: each checkpoint includes the hash of the one before it, so changing history breaks every later link. Reviewers re-check the chain in their own browser.
  • Salted fingerprints: a secret salt kept on your device stops anyone guessing your text from its hash.
  • Access control: records can be private. Share links can expire and be revoked, and teachers see only work submitted to their class.
  • Independent check: download an evidence file and verify it offline on the Independent check page.

Threat model

AttackResult
Edit an old checkpointDetected. The chain breaks.
Backdate entriesDetected, because times come from the server and must stay in order.
Server operator rewrites the whole chainPossible, unless the anchor digest was stamped with an outside service such as OpenTimestamps.
Retype AI or copied text by handNot detected. The score describes the process only.
Script that fakes keystrokesNot prevented in this version.
Someone obtains your connection codeThey can see document titles and counts. Keep it private.

Status

AuthorTrail is a working prototype. It stores data in a file on the server, has no user accounts yet, and has not had an independent security audit.
