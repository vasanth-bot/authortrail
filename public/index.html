How it works

  1. Write where you already work

    Use the built-in editor, VS Code or your browser. AuthorTrail notes what was typed, pasted and deleted, and when.

  2. Progress is sealed in checkpoints

    Each checkpoint holds a fingerprint of your work and is linked to the one before it.

  3. Share one link

    A teacher, client or publisher sees an originality score, the writing timeline and whether the record is intact. They see your text only if you choose to reveal it.

Honest by design

A record can show

  • Entries were made in order and never altered.
  • How much was typed, pasted and revised.
  • That a revealed text is a version the author worked on.

A record cannot show

  • That nobody retyped text from another source.
  • Who was at the keyboard.
  • Work done outside a tracked editor.
