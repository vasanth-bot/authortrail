Frequently asked questions

Is this an AI detector?

No. It records how text was entered (typed, pasted, revised) and reports that. It never says a text is human or AI.

Does AuthorTrail see my writing?

The record itself holds only fingerprints and counts. If Cloud save is on (it is by default in the editor), a copy of your text is also stored in your account. It is private to you but readable by whoever runs the server. Untick it to keep text on your device. The AI coach receives your draft only if you choose to send it.

How is the originality score worked out?

70% how the text was entered (typed versus pasted), 15% revision while drafting, and 15% writing pace. The verify page shows each part.

Can someone cheat it?

Yes, by retyping text from another source. The record shows effort and process, not who had the ideas. See what it proves.

What does the AI use declaration do?

You state how you used AI tools, and it appears on your record. It is your own statement and is not verified.

How do I prove a specific version is mine?

Send the reviewer your reveal file. They load it on the record page and get a clear answer on whether it matches a version you worked on.

Who can see my record?

By default, anyone with the link. You can make it private, create expiring links and revoke them on the record page.

Which tools can it track?

The built-in editor, VS Code, and text boxes in Chrome and Edge. Editors that draw their own text, such as Google Docs, may be only partly tracked.
